SkillCraft Technology Internship

Task 01: Writing Better Prompts

Domain: Prompt Engineering

Objective

To understand how clarity, specificity, context, and tone improve AI-generated responses.

Prompt 1: Artificial Intelligence

Original Prompt:
Explain Artificial Intelligence.

Improved Prompt:
Explain Artificial Intelligence in simple language for a first-year B.Tech CSE student. Include its definition, three real-world applications, and one practical example. Use headings and bullet points.

Comparison:
The original prompt is broad, while the improved prompt specifies the audience, required content, and format.

Prompt 2: Time Management

Original Prompt:
Write about time management.

Improved Prompt:
Write five practical time-management tips for a college student managing classes, assignments, and personal activities. Explain each tip in 1–2 sentences and include a sample daily study schedule.

Comparison:
The improved prompt produces more specific, actionable advice relevant to college life.

Prompt 3: Climate Change

Original Prompt:
Explain climate change.

Improved Prompt:
Explain climate change in three bullet points for a 12-year-old student. Include its main cause, two environmental effects, and one practical action students can take. Use simple language.

Comparison:
The improved prompt defines the audience, length, and topics to cover, making the response more focused.

Conclusion

Effective prompt engineering uses clear instructions, context, a defined audience, and a specific output format to obtain more relevant and useful AI-generated responses.
