SkillCraft Technology Internship

Task 02: Prompting for Creativity

Domain: Prompt Engineering

Objective

To explore how prompt structure, tone, and few-shot examples influence creative AI-generated outputs.

1. Creative Task

Generate startup ideas for college students using a structured prompt with examples.

2. Original Prompt

Give me a startup idea.

3. Improved Prompt (Few-Shot Prompting)

You are a creative startup mentor who suggests innovative, affordable business ideas for college students.

Follow these examples:

Example 1
Input: Suggest an idea related to education.
Output: StudyBuddy — a platform where students exchange notes and explain difficult topics to one another.

Example 2
Input: Suggest an idea related to sustainability.
Output: ReUse Campus — a service that helps students buy, sell, and exchange used books and college supplies.

Your Task:
Suggest three original startup ideas for college students. For each idea, provide:

1. A catchy name.
2. The problem it solves.
3. The target audience.
4. One unique feature.
5. A short, creative tagline.

Keep the ideas affordable, practical, and suitable for a college campus. Avoid repeating the examples.

4. Generated Output — Variant 1

SkillSwap Campus

- Problem: Students may need help learning skills outside their subjects.
- Target audience: College students.
- Unique feature: Students exchange skills, such as coding, graphic design, and public speaking, without paying money.
- Tagline: "Your skills are someone else's superpower."

5. Generated Output — Variant 2

MealMate

- Problem: Students often struggle to find affordable, convenient meal options.
- Target audience: Students living in hostels or away from home.
- Unique feature: A platform for sharing budget-friendly meal recommendations and coordinating group orders.
- Tagline: "Good food, less spending."

6. Generated Output — Variant 3

FocusForge

- Problem: Students struggle with distractions and maintaining study routines.
- Target audience: College students preparing for exams.
- Unique feature: Virtual study groups with timed focus sessions and progress tracking.
- Tagline: "Build focus. Shape your future."

7. Comparison of Results

Criteria| Variant 1| Variant 2| Variant 3
Tone| Collaborative| Friendly| Motivational
Creativity| Skill exchange| Shared meal planning| Gamified study habits
Detail| Clear| Clear| Clear
Practicality| High| High| High

8. Analysis

The improved prompt produces structured and relevant ideas because it specifies the audience, output format, constraints, and examples. Few-shot prompting demonstrates the expected style and level of detail. Different startup concepts also show how a single prompt can generate multiple creative directions.

Conclusion

Prompt structure and examples help guide an AI model toward more consistent, detailed, and creative outputs. Comparing multiple variants helps identify differences in tone, originality, and practical value.
